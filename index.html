Studio

A desktop application that lets its owner upload images and videos to their own Facebook Page and YouTube channel.

What the app does

Studio is a personal desktop tool. After the owner signs in with their own Facebook and Google accounts, the app uploads content that the owner has prepared to the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or process data belonging to any other person.
