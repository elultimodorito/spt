Privacy Policy

Studio · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Studio ("the app") is a desktop application used by its owner to upload content (images and videos) to the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in, Facebook and Google provide the app with authorization tokens. The app uses these tokens only to publish content to the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel. The app does not collect, read, or store personal information about any other person.

How the information is used

Authorization tokens are used for a single purpose: publishing content that the owner has chosen to upload. They are not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not transmitted to any server operated by us or to any third party.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any data to third parties.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Facebook user data

The app uses the Meta Platform only to publish content to the owner's own Facebook Page. Data received from Meta is handled in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms.

Data deletion and revoking access

You can stop the app from accessing your accounts at any time:

  • Facebook: go to Settings → Apps and Websites, find Studio, and remove it. You may also request deletion of any associated data from that screen.
  • Google / YouTube: go to https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Studio, and choose "Delete all connections".
  • Local data: uninstall the app and delete its data folder on your computer to remove all locally stored tokens.

You can also email labstudionum002@gmail.com to request deletion of any data related to you. Because the app does not store data on our servers, there is no server-side data to delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
